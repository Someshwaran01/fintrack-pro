# Privacy Policy for FinTrack Pro

**Last Updated: February 25, 2026**

FinTrack Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
FinTrack Pro is designed to be a privacy-first application. 
- **Personal Data:** We do not require you to provide personal information like your name or email to use the basic features of the app.
- **Financial Data:** All financial data (income, expenses, budgets) is stored locally on your device or synced to your private Supabase/Cloud storage if you enable sync features. We do not have access to your individual financial records.
- **AI Interactions:** If you use the Gemini AI features, your queries are processed by Google Gemini API. Please do not share sensitive personal information in AI chat prompts.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected to:
- Provide and maintain the app’s functionality.
- Synchronize your data across devices (if enabled).
- Provide AI-driven financial insights.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Local Storage:** Your data is primarily stored on your device using encrypted local storage.
- **Cloud Sync:** If enabled, data is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections to your personal database instance.
- **Security:** We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services:
- **Google Gemini API:** For AI financial assistance.
- **Supabase:** For optional data synchronization.
- **Google Play Services:** For app distribution and updates.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
